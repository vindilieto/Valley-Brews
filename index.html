Valley Brews
Built by Vincent DiLieto

Everything happening in State College this week, in one place.

Valley Brews is one weekly feed of specials, events, and promos from local bars, restaurants, shops, and hotels, posted by the businesses themselves.

Launching for Penn State students, visitors, and local businesses.

How it works

Three simple steps.

1

Businesses post

Local businesses log in and post their own specials, events, and promos. An AI assistant turns a rough note like "half off wings tuesday" into a clean post.

2

Students and visitors browse

Everyone sees one free weekly feed. Filter by specials, events, food, or game day to find what's happening tonight.

3

Businesses see results

Businesses see how many people viewed and clicked their posts, so they know what's working.

Who it's for

Built for State College, PA.

Local businesses

Bars, restaurants, shops, and hotels that want more people through the door without paying for ads that don't reach students.

Penn State students

A quick way to find deals and things to do this week, all in one feed instead of scrolling a dozen Instagram accounts.

Game-weekend visitors

Alumni, families, and fans in town for a football weekend get a game-weekend view of where to eat, drink, shop, and stay.

Why Valley Brews

What makes it worth using.

  • Always up to date. Businesses manage their own posts, so specials are current, not weeks old.
  • AI specials assistant. Managers type a quick note and get a finished post, an Instagram caption, and a text-message version.
  • Game-weekend view. One page that shows everything going on during home football weekends.
  • Free for the public. Browsing the feed is always free.
